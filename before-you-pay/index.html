About the prices, privacy & terminology license

Read the fine print first

Medication prices are API estimates for the selected item and quantity, excluding shipping and any applicable tax. Check the seller’s current product page and prescription requirements. One seller is shown; this is not a lowest-price guarantee or an insurance estimate.

Hospital records may exclude clinician, facility or other separately billed services. They are not a quote, a bill or a Good Faith Estimate. Confirm the exact procedure and get a written total from the facility. Prices do not describe quality, suitability or medical necessity.

FairVisitHealth’s free service currently allows 60 lookups per IP address per day. No automatic retries are made. Missing records are shown as missing, not filled with an invented number.

Your search & the license

This page has no account, analytics, uploads or saved search history. When you submit a search, it goes directly to the named price provider. Opening medication terminology sends that product name to NLM, and its code to CARTOS when you request verification. Those services and the hosting provider have their own privacy policies and logs. Please do not enter names, medical records or other identifying information.
